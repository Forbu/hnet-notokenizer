In computer science and artificial intelligence, hierarchical networks learn directly from raw bytes without a static tokenizer.
Instead of Byte Pair Encoding (BPE), an H-Net dynamically chunks characters into words, words into phrases, and phrases into concepts.
During autoregressive generation, the outer encoder and decoder step on every byte, while the inner causal transformer only steps when the router detects a semantic boundary.
Once upon a time, there was a little robot who learned to read books letter by letter and discovered words all on its own.
